## Citric Acid Extraction Guide

1. **Prepare Materials**: Gather citrus fruits (lemons, limes), a mortar and pestle, cheesecloth, funnel, and a clean container.
2. **Crush Fruits**: Use a mortar and pestle to crush the citrus fruits.
3. **Extract Juice**: Squeeze the crushed fruit to extract the juice, then strain through cheesecloth.
4. **Concentrate**: Heat the juice gently to evaporate water, then cool to crystallize citric acid.
5. **Collect Crystals**: Filter the mixture to collect the citric acid crystals.

*Note: This is a simplified overview. For industrial processes, specialized equipment and purification steps are required.*
